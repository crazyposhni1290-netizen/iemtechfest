About TechNova

Three days. Infinite ideas. One unforgettable experience.

The Story

TechNova is the flagship annual inter-college techno-cultural fest of the Institute of Engineering & Management (IEM), Kolkata. Born from a student idea in a hostel corridor, it has grown into East India's most electrifying celebration of technology, creativity and competition.

From 24-hour hackathons and robot combat to esports arenas, design jams and a starlit cultural night — TechNova is where the next generation of builders, designers and dreamers collide. This December, we turn the volume of innovation all the way up.
